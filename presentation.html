The Problem

  • ANSYS Rocky DEM v4.3+ implements the Tavares breakage model (Tavares et al., 2020)
  • Model requires 17 parameters for breakage probability, damage accumulation, fragment distribution
  • We need to simulate quartz sand and ceramic proppants (CarboAir, CarboLite)
  • Target: Match Hu Sun et al. (2022) fracture conductivity & breakage data
  • Critical issue: No published Tavares parameters exist for any proppant material

The Tavares Breakage Model (Rocky DEM)

Breakage Probability

  • E∞ — asymptotic fracture energy (J/kg)
  • d₀ — characteristic size (mm)
  • φ — size-effect exponent
  • σ — lognormal variance

E₅₀(d) = E∞ / [1 + (d/d₀)^φ]

Damage Accumulation

  • γ — damage accumulation coefficient
  • D = 2γ/(2γ-5) · (Eₖ/E)^(2γ/5)

Fragment Distribution

  • A — max t₁₀ (%)
  • b′ — energy sensitivity
  • αₙ/βₙ for n=1.2,1.5,2,4,25,50,75

Tavares et al. (2020), Powder Technology 362:208–220

Six Hypotheses Tested & Rejected

H1: Limestone params work for CarboAir/CarboLite

❌ Different fracture mechanics (cleavage vs. transgranular)

H2: Copper ore params work for quartz sand

❌ Different strength scale (cu ore ≫ frac sand)

H3: CarboAir & CarboLite share parameters

❌ SG 2.0 vs 2.71; different crush resistance (12% vs 17K psi)

H4: Hu Sun ceramics = CarboAir/CarboLite

❌ Chinese ceramic, density 1.43–1.46 vs 1.15/1.57

H5: Tavares size function captures size effect

⚠️ Model predicts NO size effect at proppant scale (d₀=8–100mm, d=0.2–0.4mm)

H6: γ calibrates from Mark et al. 250-day decline

❌ Static fatigue ≠ impact damage accumulation

The Core Problem: Scale Mismatch

E₅₀(d) = E∞ / [1 + (d/d₀)^φ]

Tavares Model Design

  • Comminution: 1–100 mm particles
  • d₀ = 8.07 mm (cu ore) or 100 mm (limestone)
  • Size effect activates at d ~ d₀

Proppant Reality

  • Proppants: 0.2–0.4 mm (200–400 μm)
  • d/d₀ ≈ 0.002–0.04
  • E₅₀ ≈ E∞ (constant) — NO size effect

But experiments SHOW strong size effect: 20/40 sand Br=45.68% vs 40/70 Br=30.39% (1.5×)

Research Gap: Dual-Mechanism Size Function

E₅₀(d) = E∞ / [1 + (d/d₀)^φ] × [1 + ψ·exp(-d/δ)] for d < 1 mm

Term 1: Comminution Scale

Original Tavares function (d₀ ~ 10 mm)

Term 2: Proppant Scale (NEW)

  • ψ — amplitude of proppant-scale size effect
  • δ — characteristic length (~100–300 μm)
  • Surface-flaw → volume-flaw transition

Calibration Path (Actionable)

  1. Use Man & Wong (2017) single-grain data: failure force vs. grain height (10–13 grains/type)
  2. Fit ψ and δ for sand, LD ceramic, HD ceramic
  3. Implement in Rocky DEM via user breakage model
  4. Validate against Hu Sun breakage rates at 70 MPa

Critical Adjustments to Default Parameters

  • Override E₅₀ per size class using Hu Sun breakage rates:
    • 20/40 sand: target Br=45.68% at 70 MPa → back-calculate E₅₀
    • 40/70 sand: target Br=30.39% at 70 MPa → back-calculate E₅₀
    • 20/40 ceramic: target Br=21.22% at 70 MPa
    • 40/70 ceramic: target Br=12.30% at 70 MPa
  • Differentiate Young's modulus:
    • Sand: 81 GPa (Man & Wong Baylic Sand)
    • CarboAir: ~50 GPa (Hydroprop-like)
    • CarboLite: ~130 GPa (OxSteel-like)
  • Contact model: Hysteretic linear spring (Tavares et al. 2020)
  • Friction: p-p 0.8, p-steel 0.5; Restitution 0.3
  • Min progeny: 1/22 parent size; Polyhedral, 25 corners, superquadric degree 3.0
